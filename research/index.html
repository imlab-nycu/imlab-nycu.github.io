Research highlight

Listening to Microdroplets: Acoustic Signatures for Microfluidic Sensing

How can the generation state and characteristics of microdroplets be identified in a microfluidic device? IMLAB explores acoustic signatures as a source of sensing information. Here, “voiceprint” refers to acoustic features associated with droplet generation—not human speaker identification.

A 2024 study investigated voiceprint-based sensing of droplet generation and mode transitions in a coaxial microfluidic device. A 2026 study combined voiceprint features with a convolutional neural network (CNN) for label-free identification of sodium-alginate microdroplet size and concentration during high-frequency generation. Each study should be read within its own experimental conditions and validation scope.

聽見微液滴：以聲紋探索生成狀態與液滴特徵

微液滴在微流體裝置中生成時，如何辨識其生成狀態與特徵，是微流體量測的重要課題。IMLAB 探索以聲紋作為感測資訊：此處的「聲紋」指與液滴生成相關的聲學特徵，而非人聲身分辨識。

2024 年的研究探討同軸微流體裝置中的液滴生成與模式轉換感測；2026 年的研究進一步結合聲紋特徵與卷積神經網路（CNN），用於高頻生成過程中海藻酸鈉微液滴尺寸及濃度的無標記辨識。各自適用條件與驗證範圍仍以原論文為準。上圖為概念示意，非實驗裝置照片或量測結果。
