Megumi Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Google User Data Access

Megumi may access Google user data needed to assist its authorized user: Gmail data necessary for email assistance, Google Calendar data for calendar assistance, and Google Drive data for file assistance.

Use of Google User Data

Google user data is used only to provide functions explicitly requested by the authorized user.

Data Sharing and Advertising

Google user data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Access Scope

Access is limited to the authorized personal-use account. The user can revoke Google account access at any time through their Google Account permissions.
