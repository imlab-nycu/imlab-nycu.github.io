Megumi

Personal AI assistant for authorized use.

Megumi is a personal AI assistant operated by the Intelligent Microsystems Laboratory.

About Megumi

Megumi assists its authorized user with email, calendar, and file-management tasks. It uses Google APIs, including Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive, to provide requested assistance for the authorized personal-use account.

Megumi is a private/personal-use application and is not offered as a public service.
